Compliance Glossary & Reference

The definitive reference guide for compliance officers, revenue cycle leaders, and technical teams navigating CMS Hospital Price Transparency regulations.

The CFO Hospital Price Transparency Glossary

Percentage-Based Methodology

A standard charge established as a percentage of a referenced benchmark. Encode a dollar amount when the charge can be expressed as one. For qualifying percentage or algorithm arrangements that cannot be expressed as a dollar amount or are not knowable in advance, disclose the applicable method and available information needed to derive the amount, and apply the current allowed-amount reporting guidance. Sources: 45 CFR 180.50; CMS FAQ: June 26, 2026.

Penny Rates ($0.01)

A one-cent value recorded as a charge. The amount alone does not establish that it is a placeholder or that the hospital is noncompliant. Check the applicable charge field and supporting source record before classifying the value. Sources: 45 CFR 180.50.

Payer/Plan Mapping

The association of each applicable payer-specific negotiated charge with the correct third-party payer and plan. The rule requires that association. CMS's Online Validator checks form and manner; a validator result does not establish that the mapping agrees with the hospital's source contracts or rate data. Sources: 45 CFR 180.50; CMS Online Validator: scope and dictionary versions.

Machine-Readable Version 2.0

An earlier generation of CMS's standardized machine-readable-file specifications. Version numbers identify the dictionary and requirements being tested; they are not interchangeable. The current CMS Online Validator lists v3.0 for requirements effective January 1, 2026 and enforced April 1, 2026, alongside earlier v2.2 and v2.1 options. Sources: CMS Online Validator: scope and dictionary versions.

Regulatory and Enforcement Glossary

45 CFR Part 180

The section of the Code of Federal Regulations that codifies the Hospital Price Transparency requirements, establishing the legal obligations for hospitals to make standard charges public.

Affirmation Statement

The earlier in-file affirmation provisions applied from January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2025. Beginning January 1, 2026, the modified attestation includes dollar-amount and derivability language, and the file must name the CEO, president or designated senior official. CMS delayed enforcement of these new MRF requirements until April 1, 2026. Use the applicable CSV or JSON layout; this is not a separate CMS certification. Sources: 45 CFR 180.50; CMS Online Validator: scope and dictionary versions; CMS: CY 2026 final-rule changes.

Civil Monetary Penalty (CMP)

A financial penalty CMS may impose under its hospital price transparency enforcement process. The adjusted daily maximums carried forward for 2026 are $342 for hospitals with 30 or fewer beds, $11 per bed for 31-550 beds, and $6,277 for more than 550 beds. Use the applicable notice and assessment period for an actual case amount. Sources: 45 CFR 102.3: adjusted maximums; HHS: no 2026 inflation adjustment.

Comprehensive Machine-Readable File

A single digital file that must contain all standard charges (gross, payer-specific, de-identified min/max, discounted cash) for all items and services provided by the hospital.

Monitoring and Enforcement

The CMS process of auditing hospital websites and MRFs. It involves automated scans, manual reviews, warning notices, requests for corrective action plans (CAP), and imposition of penalties.

Data Quality and Forbidden Patterns Glossary

Forbidden Rates (Placeholder Rates)

Values inserted in place of the applicable actual standard-charge information. A suspected placeholder needs source reconciliation and field-specific review. Do not treat every zero or blank as an automatic failure: the correct encoding depends on the data element and the facts described in CMS guidance. Sources: 45 CFR 180.50; CMS FAQ: June 26, 2026.

Ghost Rates

Rates that appear in the file but correspond to services the hospital does not actually provide or for payers they do not contract with, creating 'noise' in the data.

Schema Validation

The technical process of verifying that the MRF JSON or CSV structure strictly adheres to the CMS-mandated template, ensuring parsability by aggregators and regulators.

Data Integrity Score

A metric used by YieldGuard to quantify the reliability of an MRF, calculating the ratio of valid, transactable rates versus placeholders, zeros, or malformed entries.

Financial and Payer Glossary

Negotiated Charge

The charge that a third-party payer and hospital have contractually agreed upon for an item or service.

De-identified Maximum

The highest negotiated charge that a hospital has agreed to with all third-party payers for an item or service, presented without identifying the specific payer.

De-identified Minimum

The lowest negotiated charge that a hospital has agreed to with all third-party payers for an item or service, presented without identifying the specific payer.

Discounted Cash Price

The charge that applies to an individual who pays cash (or cash equivalent) for a hospital item or service, unrelated to any insurance plan.

Payer-Specific Negotiated Charge

The charge that a hospital has negotiated with a specific third-party payer for an item or service, which must be clearly linked to the payer's name and plan.

Payer Parity

Analysis comparing negotiated rates across different payers for the same service, often revealing discrepancies in contract performance and revenue leakage.

Technical and Large Scale Processing Glossary

JSON (JavaScript Object Notation)

One of the formats supported by CMS's standardized MRF specifications. CMS also provides a CSV layout. Use the prescribed layout, data specifications and dictionary for the selected format; JSON alone is not the only permitted format. Sources: 45 CFR 180.50; CMS Online Validator: scope and dictionary versions.

Streaming Parser (NDJSON)

A method of reading very large files (like 500MB+ MRFs) line-by-line or chunk-by-chunk, preventing memory crashes common with standard JSON parsers.

CDM (Charge Description Master)

The comprehensive list of items and services billable to a patient or a patient's health insurance provider, serving as the foundational dataset for MRF generation.

NPI (National Provider Identifier)

A unique 10-digit identification number issued to health care providers in the United States by CMS, required in the file header to identify the hospital entity.

CPT / HCPCS / NDC

Standardized medical coding systems (Current Procedural Terminology, Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System, National Drug Code) required to identify items and services in the MRF.
