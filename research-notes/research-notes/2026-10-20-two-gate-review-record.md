# Research note: two-gate review record

Proposed two-gate review record: gate 1, structural validation result (tool, version, date, pass or fail). Gate 2, evidence review of flagged rows (selection rule, source comparison, outcome). A pass at gate 1 never substitutes for gate 2. Record both gates so a later reader can tell a well-formed file from an accurate one. This note describes review design, not a compliance certification.
