# Research note: finding revalidation record

Proposed revalidation record: original retrieval date; finding version; date re-checked; source re-downloaded (yes or no); checksum comparison result; candidate counts before and after; outcome (confirmed, revised, retired). A finding cited after its source may have changed carries its last revalidation date. No revalidation, no reuse. This note describes evidence maintenance, not a new finding.
