# Research note: retest record

Proposed retest record: original row reference; original values; reporting-applicability decision; source evidence reviewed; supported field changes; reviewer outcome; post-change check result. Do not automatically patch count or statistics from the detector. The checker produces candidates; the data owner reconciles the source; the retest verifies the supported change.
