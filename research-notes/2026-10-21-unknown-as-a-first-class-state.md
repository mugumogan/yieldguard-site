# Research note: UNKNOWN as a first-class state

Proposed review states: CANDIDATE (matches the detection rule), APPLICABLE (allowed-amount reporting applies), NOT_APPLICABLE (fully calculable dollar case or equivalent), UNKNOWN (public data cannot settle it), RESOLVED (evidence reviewed and outcome recorded). Report UNKNOWN counts openly. Do not convert UNKNOWN to pass or fail without source evidence. This note describes review workflow, not a compliance verdict.
