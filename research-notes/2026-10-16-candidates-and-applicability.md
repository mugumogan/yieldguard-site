# Research note: separate candidate detection and applicability

Suggested two-stage design. Stage 1 retains rows with a percentage or algorithm and a zero count plus populated statistics as candidates. Stage 2 resolves whether allowed-amount reporting applies, including the fully-calculable-dollar exception. Keep UNKNOWN as a review state when the public file cannot settle it. Never label all Stage 1 matches as violations. This is proposed review logic, not executable compliance certification.
