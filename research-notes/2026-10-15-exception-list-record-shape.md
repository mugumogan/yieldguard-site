# Research note: exception-list record shape

Proposed fields: source row reference; payer/plan context; charge method; negotiated dollar/percentage/algorithm values; count; median; p10; p90; notes; applicability decision; review outcome. Keep source identifiers in the private operational review, not in a public fixture. The public note documents the record shape only. An exception record is a review candidate, not proof of exporter causation or wrong pricing.
