# Research note: count files and findings separately

November 16, 2026

Inventory design: source_file_id is the parent key; finding_id identifies a condition attached to that source. Report distinct source_file_id and finding_id counts separately. The published October inventory has 179 source files and 183 finding records; this is not a prevalence denominator.
