# Research note: comparison preconditions

Proposed comparison preconditions: for each input, record unit (coded row, service, charge, hospital), selection rule, source scope and retrieval date. Align units before arithmetic; document pairs that cannot be aligned and exclude them. Never publish a cross-source delta whose two sides count different things. This note describes comparison hygiene, not a benchmark result.
