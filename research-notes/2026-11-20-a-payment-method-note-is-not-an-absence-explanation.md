# Research note: a payment-method note is not an absence explanation

November 20, 2026

Separate tests for qualifying non-dollar arrangements: formula/method information; no-remittance explanation; count/statistic relationship. For file #122 the recorded six-entry finding concerns the explanation, while the allowed-amount statistics were blank. Do not reclassify it as inverse encoding.
