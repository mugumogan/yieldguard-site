# Research note: human disposition record

Proposed disposition record: candidate ID; detection rule and reason; reviewer role; decision (confirmed issue, explained, not applicable, unknown); evidence reference; decision date. Automated systems propose candidates; accountable people record dispositions. Do not let the detector write the final state. This note describes workflow design, not staffing advice.
