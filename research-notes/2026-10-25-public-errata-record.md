# Research note: public errata record

Proposed errata record: finding version; date; section changed; before and after text or values; reason; reviewer. Corrections to published findings are appended, never silently replaced. Keep superseded versions addressable so earlier citations remain checkable. This note describes publication hygiene for research artifacts.
