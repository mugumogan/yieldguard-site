Common Technical Risks We Detect

Our diagnostic tools frequently identify these specific technical failures that generic IT processes miss.

Data Corruption

THE RISK

Export errors, such as Scientific Notation, render price data mathematically unreadable for automated systems. This creates a technical violation of digital accessibility standards.

YIELDGUARD SOLUTION

YieldGuard's pre-validation engine detects formatting anomalies like scientific notation and enforces strict schema compliance before any data is published.

Hidden Gaps

THE RISK

Static placeholders like 999,999,999 hide actual rates and are flagged as bad-faith omissions by federal auditors. These gaps prevent accurate price comparisons and trigger regulatory reviews.

YIELDGUARD SOLUTION

We automatically scan for and flag high-risk placeholder values, replacing them with compliant null indicators or valid calculated rates based on contract logic.

Estimative Gaps

THE RISK

New mandates require hospitals to convert percentage-based contracts into clear, dollar-denominated estimates. Files lacking these calculated values are now considered incomplete under current enforcement rules.

YIELDGUARD SOLUTION

Our system algorithmically derives the required dollar-denominated estimates for percentage-based contracts, ensuring every line item has a compliant value.
