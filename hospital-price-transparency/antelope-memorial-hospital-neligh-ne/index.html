Financial and historical data: NASHP Hospital Cost Tool, dataset released December 30, 2025, produced with Rice University's Baker Institute; underlying sources include CMS hospital cost reports. Matched to CCN 281326; source name ANTELOPE MEMORIAL HOSPITAL. Figures describe the Medicare cost-report reporting entity, not necessarily an individual campus. Missing and invalid source values are omitted from the latest tables and left blank within the historical series.
