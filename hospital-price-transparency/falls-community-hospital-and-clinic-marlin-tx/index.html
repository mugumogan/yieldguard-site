Sources and notes

Bed count source: Certified beds, CMS provider data, Q2 2026

Adult community health estimates, not diagnoses or patients treated at this hospital. Age-adjusted prevalence (%) among adults; CDC PLACES 2025 release, BRFSS 2023.

Conditions with higher county estimates and non-overlapping displayed 95% confidence intervals versus the CDC U.S. reference:

County estimates are modeled community prevalence, not this hospital's caseload. The U.S. values are CDC reference estimates from the same release/year/measure and age-adjusted series. Displayed 95% confidence intervals do not overlap for the listed conditions, but this is not a formal significance test or a diagnosis. No overall health ranking is implied. Differences are percentage points (pp), not relative percentages. CDC PLACES data · Comparison guidance.

Institution profile, address and phone: CMS Hospital General Information, released 2026-11-12. Matched by CMS certification number; directory and source names can differ.

Financial and historical data: NASHP Hospital Cost Tool, dataset released December 30, 2025, produced with Rice University's Baker Institute; underlying sources include CMS hospital cost reports. Matched to CCN 670780; source name FALLS COMMUNITY HOSPITAL AND CLINIC. Figures describe the Medicare cost-report reporting entity, not necessarily an individual campus. Years are fiscal years ending in the stated year; dollar values are nominal, not inflation-adjusted. Payer mix is the share of hospital charges, not patients, discharges, revenue or insurance enrollment. Commercial includes other payer categories such as TriCare and VA and is not employer-only coverage. NASHP net margin is net income divided by net patient revenue; its patient-care operating margin uses net patient revenue less Medicare-allowable hospital operating costs, not total operating expenses. These definitions differ from other financial filings shown on this page; do not splice them into one trend. Net charity care cost is not total community benefit. Missing and invalid source values are omitted from the latest tables and left blank within the historical series. Chart gaps represent missing years or values, not zero; each metric has its own scale. Changes are calculated before rounding. Historical values do not control for acquisitions, service changes or accounting changes. NASHP flags multiple cost reports in at least one displayed fiscal year; those periods may not be directly comparable. Source checked November 15, 2026.

Annual revenue, beds and activity: CMS Hospital Provider Cost Report; CMS definitions. Values describe the cost-report reporting entity, which may include multiple campuses. Net patient revenue is reported revenue for the stated fiscal period, not a monthly run rate or a projected annualized estimate. Only 365- or 366-day reports are shown; shorter, longer, ambiguous or identity-conflicting periods are omitted. Beds are adult and pediatric hospital beds available at period end, not CMS-certified beds from the directory header. Discharges are adult/pediatric inpatient discharge episodes, including deaths and excluding newborns/dead-on-arrival; they are not unique patients or outpatient encounters. Missing values are blank and chart gaps, never zero. Each metric has its own scale. CMS and NASHP figures are separate series and may reflect different report selections or revisions; do not splice them. Sources are checked weekly for new annual releases and revisions, not new monthly observations. Dollar values are nominal. Exact reporting periods: 2024: 2023-09-01 to 2024-08-31 (CMS report 796802); 2025: 2024-09-01 to 2025-08-31 (CMS report 829048).
