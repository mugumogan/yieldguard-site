Sources and notes

Bed count source: Certified beds, CMS provider data, Q2 2026

Discharges are inpatient episodes, not unique people or total patients treated. Outpatient visits are not included. No annualization. Includes deaths, excludes newborns and deaths on arrival. CMS cost reports, file year 2024. This is not a count of all people treated by the hospital.

Institution profile, address and phone: CMS Hospital General Information, released 2026-11-12. Matched by CMS certification number; directory and source names can differ.

Annual revenue, beds and activity: CMS Hospital Provider Cost Report; CMS definitions. Values describe the cost-report reporting entity, which may include multiple campuses. Net patient revenue is reported revenue for the stated fiscal period, not a monthly run rate or a projected annualized estimate. Only 365- or 366-day reports are shown; shorter, longer, ambiguous or identity-conflicting periods are omitted. Beds are adult and pediatric hospital beds available at period end, not CMS-certified beds from the directory header. Discharges are adult/pediatric inpatient discharge episodes, including deaths and excluding newborns/dead-on-arrival; they are not unique patients or outpatient encounters. Missing values are blank and chart gaps, never zero. Each metric has its own scale. CMS and NASHP figures are separate series and may reflect different report selections or revisions; do not splice them. Sources are checked weekly for new annual releases and revisions, not new monthly observations. Dollar values are nominal. Exact reporting periods: 2012: 2011-07-01 to 2012-06-30 (CMS report 784552); 2013: 2012-07-01 to 2013-06-30 (CMS report 784553); 2014: 2013-07-01 to 2014-06-30 (CMS report 784554); 2015: 2014-07-01 to 2015-06-30 (CMS report 625057); 2016: 2015-07-01 to 2016-06-30 (CMS report 632064); 2017: 2016-07-01 to 2017-06-30 (CMS report 653803); 2018: 2017-07-01 to 2018-06-30 (CMS report 736316); 2019: 2018-07-01 to 2019-06-30 (CMS report 723421); 2020: 2019-07-01 to 2020-06-30 (CMS report 758730); 2021: 2020-07-01 to 2021-06-30 (CMS report 767348); 2022: 2021-07-01 to 2022-06-30 (CMS report 793545); 2023: 2022-07-01 to 2023-06-30 (CMS report 820299); 2024: 2023-07-01 to 2024-06-30 (CMS report 828699); 2025: 2024-07-01 to 2025-06-30 (CMS report 824671).
