Financial and historical data: NASHP Hospital Cost Tool, dataset released December 30, 2025, produced with Rice University's Baker Institute; underlying sources include CMS hospital cost reports. Matched to CCN 140065; source name UCM ADVENTHEALTH LAGRANGE. Figures describe the Medicare cost-report reporting entity, not necessarily an individual campus. Years are fiscal years ending in the stated year; dollar values are nominal, not inflation-adjusted. Payer mix is the share of hospital charges, not patients, discharges, revenue or insurance enrollment. Commercial includes other payer categories such as TriCare and VA and is not employer-only coverage. NASHP net margin is net income divided by net patient revenue; its patient-care operating margin uses net patient revenue less Medicare-allowable hospital operating costs, not total operating expenses. Missing and invalid source values are omitted from the latest tables and left blank within the historical series. Chart gaps represent missing years or values, not zero; each metric has its own scale. Changes are calculated before rounding. Historical values do not control for acquisitions, service changes or accounting changes.
