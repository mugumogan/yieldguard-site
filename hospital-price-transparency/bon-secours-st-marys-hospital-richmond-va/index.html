Sources and notes

Bed count source: Certified beds, CMS provider data, Q2 2026

Institution profile, address and phone: CMS Hospital General Information, released 2026-11-12. Matched by CMS certification number; directory and source names can differ.

Financial and historical data: NASHP Hospital Cost Tool, dataset released December 30, 2025, produced with Rice University's Baker Institute; underlying sources include CMS hospital cost reports. Matched to CCN 490059; source name BON SECOURS - ST. MARYS HOSPITAL. Figures describe the Medicare cost-report reporting entity, not necessarily an individual campus. Years are fiscal years ending in the stated year; dollar values are nominal, not inflation-adjusted. Payer mix is the share of hospital charges, not patients, discharges, revenue or insurance enrollment. Commercial includes other payer categories such as TriCare and VA and is not employer-only coverage. NASHP net margin is net income divided by net patient revenue; its patient-care operating margin uses net patient revenue less Medicare-allowable hospital operating costs, not total operating expenses. These definitions differ from other financial filings shown on this page; do not splice them into one trend. Net charity care cost is not total community benefit. Missing and invalid source values are omitted from the latest tables and left blank within the historical series. Changes are calculated before rounding. Historical values do not control for acquisitions, service changes or accounting changes. NASHP flags multiple cost reports in at least one displayed fiscal year; those periods may not be directly comparable. Source checked November 15, 2026.
