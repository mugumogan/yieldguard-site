County estimates are modeled community prevalence, not this hospital's caseload. The U.S. values are CDC reference estimates from the same release/year/measure and age-adjusted series. Institution profile, address and phone: CMS Hospital General Information, released 2026-11-12. Matched by CMS certification number; directory and source names can differ.

Annual revenue, beds and activity: CMS Hospital Provider Cost Report; CMS definitions. Values describe the cost-report reporting entity, which may include multiple campuses. Net patient revenue is reported revenue for the stated fiscal period, not a monthly run rate or a projected annualized estimate. Only 365- or 366-day reports are shown; shorter, longer, ambiguous or identity-conflicting periods are omitted. Beds are adult and pediatric hospital beds available at period end, not CMS-certified beds from the directory header. Discharges are adult/pediatric inpatient discharge episodes, including deaths and excluding newborns/dead-on-arrival; they are not unique patients or outpatient encounters. Missing values are blank and chart gaps, never zero. Each metric has its own scale. CMS and NASHP figures are separate series and may reflect different report selections or revisions; do not splice them. Sources are checked weekly for new annual releases and revisions, not new monthly observations. Dollar values are nominal.
