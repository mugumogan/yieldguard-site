THE 2025 LANDSCAPE SHIFT

For years, hospital price transparency was an exercise in "good faith." Files were posted, boxes were checked, and enforcement was rare.

That era ended with Executive Order 14221.

We watched as the regulatory landscape shifted overnight from passive observation to algorithmic enforcement. We realized that traditional revenue cycle management (RCM) tools were ill-equipped for a world where a single metadata mismatch could trigger a six-figure penalty.

YieldGuard was born from a simple premise: Compliance shouldn't require a crisis. We built the automated defense layer that hospitals needed but didn't have the internal engineering resources to construct.
