An unknown denominator is not zero

November 18, 2026 · Stored October 2026 audit

Stored file #116 reports a unique affected-entry union of 6,233 across multiple finding classes. Its preserved audit record does not supply an applicable denominator. The published review therefore reports no affected share.

Neither zero nor the total affected population can stand in for the missing denominator. Dividing by the affected count would manufacture a 100% result; substituting another file's population would answer a different question.

A useful report can still state the known count and describe the finding classes. Mark the applicable population and share as not recorded, then recover and verify that population before calculating a rate. This is a reporting recommendation based on the evidence limit, not an additional CMS field requirement.
