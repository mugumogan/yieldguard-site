Show six entries without exaggerating the chart

November 19, 2026 · Stored October 2026 audit

Stored file #122 has six missing-explanation entries among 48,741 applicable entries. The published share is 0.012%, rounded. A proportional bar is almost invisible at that scale, but a chart should not enlarge it into a visually substantial share.

Write both counts beside the chart and label the rounded percentage. A tiny mark plus an exact count conveys the result more honestly than a minimum-width bar that appears to represent a larger portion of the population.

The six-entry finding is still a recorded issue to review; its small share neither excuses it nor establishes its severity. The October audit describes a missing no-remittance explanation, not inverse encoding, a current endpoint result or a calculated financial effect.

Sources and notes
