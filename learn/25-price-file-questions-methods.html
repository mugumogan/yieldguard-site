1 · Cash comparison

A posted-line comparison, with each line weighted once

Two same-system retained originals, both CMS template v2.2.0 and declaring December 1, 2025. Site identities and raw prices stay out of this pack. Keep one CPT or HCPCS code per item, exclude drug-information items and modifiers at both item and charge level, and require code, code type, setting and facility billing class to match. Of 1,711 shared keys, 643 are multivalued; 1,068 are single-valued in both files. Ten have different description sets, leaving 1,058 positive exact-description matches.

For each retained key, calculate Site B cash / Site A cash. The reported 16.2% difference is 100 x (median of those ratios - 1), not a ratio of cohort medians and not a spend-weighted saving. Every distinct gross value attached to all 1,058 matched keys in both files gives the posted cash figure at 55% of gross within half-cent rounding. Identical charge-line descriptions support comparison of the posted lines; they do not promise identical clinical episodes or a complete patient bill. Removing 12 generic-description keys leaves 1,046 pairs and a 16.3% median difference.

The earlier 1,055 count is replaced, not incremented: 1,036 prior matches remain, 19 drop and 22 become eligible when modifier-bearing charge objects are removed before grouping. Some mixed keys become unambiguous after their modifier variants are removed. Independent clinician fees may be additional; negotiated and allowed amounts are not personalized out-of-pocket costs.

2 · Inpatient estimates and service priority

Keep the price types and payer populations separate

The estimate comparison matches MS-DRG, exact payer-plan labels, inpatient setting, facility billing class, methodology, algorithm text and percentage where present. Retain one positive estimated amount and the same description set in each file. This yields 105 pairs across 49 DRGs and 12 payer-plan groups. The estimates reflect algorithm-based payment and utilization, not a negotiated dollar fee schedule. The MRFs do not declare a DRG version; these are exact-code/description review groups, not a claim that all case mix is equal.

CMS 2024 provider/service rows join to the two retained MRFs by CCN, with provider name, street address, city and state agreement. Site A contributes 18 published DRG rows and 448 discharges; Site B contributes nine and 245. CMS suppresses provider/DRG cells with ten or fewer discharges. The 693 total is therefore published Original Medicare fee-for-service volume, not all hospital volume. Sepsis 871, heart failure 291 and pneumonia 193 contribute 346 of those discharges. Do not multiply these fee-for-service counts by a commercial or Medicare Advantage plan's estimated amount to claim revenue opportunity.

3 · Existing research cohorts

Use the denominator that belongs to the question

The October 2026 research sweep covers 1,282 CY2026 files and 183 findings on 179 files. Finding families overlap; the 25.0 million affected rows belong to the inverse-encoding findings, not every file reviewed. The 66-file deep re-scan, 257-file header cohort, 5,421-hospital directory and 76-file own-check audit are separate populations. The financial join is exploratory and includes 24 name-and-location-confirmed matches; hosting-platform concentration prevents a causal financial claim.

CMS enforcement results were retrieved November 18, 2026: 13,355 actions, 8,038 cases and 3,646 warned cases. CMS's disclosed universe is closed-case based; a missing closure notice does not establish an open case, and these paths do not provide prospective penalty probabilities. The 99-day median measures warning-to-closure notices, not internal repair labor.

The earlier cash pilot inspected four files, three entering matched-price comparisons; the nearest independent could not be matched. Its 93% median difference compared a real discount schedule with gross-equal cash fields. It is not used as a competitive cash benchmark here.

4 · Decision models and remaining inputs

Judgment is explicit; missing observations stay missing

Triage order, release gates, board measures and operating-model recommendations are our analytical judgment informed by the measured findings. Revenue scenarios are arithmetic under stated assumptions, not hospital forecasts. Total revenue change equals the assumed affected revenue share x ((1 + rate change) x (1 + eligible volume change) - 1). Break-even eligible-volume loss is rate change / (1 + rate change).

Four empirical tests still lack their necessary observations: a complete approved-version history (Q6), a paired payer-side file (Q18), a correction-to-next-release series (Q19), and hospital/vendor cost inputs (Q22). The pack gives a decision rule for each rather than inventing a result. Contract completeness and remittance reproduction also require their underlying hospital records. The working inventory is two fully supported answers, nineteen partial answers and four not-yet-measured tests; those labels are not the conclusions.
