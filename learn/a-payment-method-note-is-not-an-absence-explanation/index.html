A payment-method note is not an absence explanation

November 20, 2026 · Stored October 2026 audit

The six affected entries in stored file #122 had count zero and blank allowed-amount statistics. Their generic notes described payment methods, including OPPS APC or packaged-code logic. The recorded finding was that those notes did not explain the absence of remittances.

A formula answers how payment is determined. An absence explanation answers why the applicable lookback supplies no allowed amounts. Those are different pieces of information. CMS's no-claims guidance for qualifying non-dollar arrangements describes count zero, blank allowed-amount statistics and an explanation when its conditions apply.

The same stored review kept 41,187 entries with explicit prior-12-month zero-final-payment explanations outside this six-entry gap. That exclusion does not verify the truth or adequacy of every other note. It keeps this finding confined to the absence-explanation test actually recorded.
