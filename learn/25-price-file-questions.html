The cash-price answer

One system. A 16.2% posted cash-price gap.

Both hospitals apply the same 45% cash discount on every matched key. Site B is nevertheless lower on 734 of 1,058 matched facility cash items, equal on 64, and higher on 260. The median paired B/A ratio is 0.8378. The decision: review the underlying gross-price lines, not the headline discount percentage.

The reimbursement answer

Start with sepsis, heart failure and pneumonia.

Three DRGs account for 346 of 693 discharges, or 49.9%, in the two hospitals' published 2024 Original Medicare rows. They also have matched payer-plan estimated amounts in the retained price files. That puts a real volume order behind the first reimbursement reviews.

The inpatient comparison does not show a blanket hospital premium. Across 105 matched DRG/payer-plan groups the median Site B/A estimated-amount index is 100.4, but the largest matched plan group is 122.8. Work by service and contract group.

The control answer

Fix contradictory statistics before chasing strange prices.

The 1,282-file sweep found 179 files with semantic findings. Its dominant issue, zero-count rows carrying claim-derived statistics, accounts for 137 findings and 25.0 million affected published rows. Those belong ahead of generic price-outlier screens in a hospital's work queue.
